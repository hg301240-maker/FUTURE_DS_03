# 📊 Marketing Conversion Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Marketing Conversion Analytics Dashboard** created using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard analyzes the complete marketing lead journey, from **Lead Generation to Purchase**, and provides insights into lead volume, conversion performance, marketing channels, regions, lead stages, and monthly trends.

The dashboard helps understand how marketing leads are progressing through different stages and which channels and regions are contributing to the overall performance.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze the total number of marketing leads.
* Monitor lead-to-customer conversion.
* Track the lead generation trend over time.
* Analyze leads by marketing channel.
* Analyze purchases by marketing channel.
* Compare lead performance across different regions.
* Analyze leads based on the highest stage reached.
* Identify important conversion patterns.
* Provide an interactive dashboard for marketing performance analysis.

---

## 📊 Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard includes important KPI cards such as:

* **Total Leads**
* **Lead-to-Customer Conversion**
* **Purchases**
* **Lead Count**

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the overall marketing performance.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Lead Generation Trend

A monthly line chart is used to analyze the **Lead Generation Trend**.

This helps identify:

* Monthly changes in lead generation
* Periods of higher lead activity
* Periods of lower lead activity
* Overall lead generation patterns

---

### 2. Leads by Marketing Channel

The dashboard compares the number of leads generated through different **Marketing Channels**.

This helps businesses understand:

* Which channels generate more leads
* How lead generation varies between channels
* Which channels require further analysis

---

### 3. Purchases by Marketing Channel

A separate column chart analyzes **Purchases by Marketing Channel**.

This allows comparison between lead generation and actual purchases and helps evaluate how different channels contribute to the final conversion stage.

---

### 4. Lead Stage Analysis

A funnel chart is used to analyze the number of leads at different stages of the marketing journey.

The analysis is based on:

* Lead
* Contacted
* Qualified
* Proposal
* Negotiation
* Purchase

This provides a visual representation of how leads move through the conversion funnel.

---

### 5. Highest Stage Reached

A pie chart shows the distribution of leads according to their **Highest Stage Reached**.

This helps identify the stages where leads are concentrated and understand the overall progression of leads.

---

### 6. Regional Analysis

The dashboard includes a regional analysis of leads.

The region-based visual helps compare marketing performance across different geographical regions.

A **Region slicer** is also provided so users can interactively filter the dashboard.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:

### 🌎 Region

Users can select a specific region to analyze its marketing performance.

### 📢 Marketing Channel

Users can filter the dashboard according to a particular marketing channel.

### 📅 Month

Users can select a month to analyze the corresponding lead and marketing performance.

These filters make the dashboard interactive and easy to explore.

---

## 🔄 Marketing Conversion Funnel

The dashboard represents the marketing journey as:

**Lead → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Purchase**

This funnel helps identify how leads progress from initial generation to the final purchase stage.
